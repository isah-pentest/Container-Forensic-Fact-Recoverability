## Decision 1 — store capacity M_q, k_q (2026-10-26, before any Q run)
Inputs: load map `~/frx-evidence/qualification/load_map.json` (metadata only, 20 replicas of
`python@sha256:82abc9f5…508c`, 60 s per setting, 2×2 + centre design).

| Fit | Intercept | Per unit | Cross term | r² |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| audit vs total API GET/s | 3,538 B/s | 1,347 B | −0.36 B per exec | 0.999999 |
| Tetragon vs total exec/s | 18.5 B/s | 4,708 B | 0.39 B per GET | 0.9999997 |

Tested envelope: 400 GET/s and 200 exec/s together, no errors.

Operating points by the Section 4.3 rule (δ = 60 s, action 8 s, exec 5 s after ACTION_REQUEST):

| Store (both sources) | r_M | Highest rate (H ≈ 0.5) | In envelope | Loss ramp M/r at r_M |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 MiB × 5 | 627 GET/s, 194 exec/s | 1,257 GET/s, 389 exec/s | no | 12 s |
| 10 MiB × 3 | 398 GET/s, 124 exec/s | 799 GET/s, 247 exec/s | no | 19 s |
| 10 MiB × 2 | 284 GET/s, 88 exec/s | 570 GET/s, 177 exec/s | no | 27 s |
| **10 MiB × 1** | 169 GET/s, 53 exec/s | 341 GET/s, 106 exec/s | **yes** | 45 s |

**Decision: 10 MiB × 1 for both sources** (author's choice 2026-10-26). Rationale:
M = 10 MiB: Tetragon Helm chart 1.7.1 default export file size, used for both sources. k reduced from the chart default 5 to 1 under Section 4.3 item 4: the largest k whose highest required rate (Q-c and calibration, H ~ 0.5: 341 API GET/s, 106 exec/s) lies inside the envelope measured in load_map.json (400 GET/s, 200 exec/s); k = 5, 3, 2 need 1257, 799, 570 GET/s. Decided from load-map metadata only, before any Q run.
A wider loss ramp also keeps r_M's 0.3–0.7 loss probability robust to run-to-run variation in
action duration (about ±0.04 per ±2 s at k = 1, against ±0.16 at k = 5).
Scope consequence (stated in the manuscript): the 2×2 is estimated at a 20 MiB store per source;
the calibration series covers H from 2 to 0.5.
